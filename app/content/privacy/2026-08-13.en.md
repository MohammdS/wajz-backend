# Wajz Privacy Notice

Wajz uses first-party analytics to understand whether the app is reliable and useful. We record limited events such as opening a story, saving it, completing a briefing, and following a source link.

We do not put article text, source URLs, email addresses, internal prompts, or IP addresses in analytics events. Guest activity uses a random installation identifier. If you later sign in, only future activity is linked to your account.

Detailed events are kept for 90 days. Deidentified daily totals are kept for 13 months. You can withdraw this acknowledgement or delete associated analytics data from Privacy settings. Withdrawing blocks app use until the current notice is accepted again.

This notice is product copy and must receive legal review before production launch.
